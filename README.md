# 🌿 ROOTS — Bringing Indian Heritage into Modern Everyday Life

> **Where heritage meets modern living.**

## 📌 Overview

ROOTS is an education-first digital experience and e-commerce platform designed to reconnect modern consumers with India's traditional household products and the stories behind them.

Traditional materials such as copper, brass, and bronze have been part of Indian households for generations. However, modern consumers often encounter these products simply as commodities, without understanding their cultural significance, craftsmanship, traditional uses, maintenance, or the evidence behind associated wellness claims.

ROOTS brings these aspects together through a clean, premium and contemporary digital experience, complemented by sustainable, story-driven packaging.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Traditional Indian household products carry cultural knowledge, craftsmanship and history that can easily be lost when they are presented only as commercial products.

Consumers often face several challenges:

- Limited understanding of the history and cultural significance of traditional materials
- Difficulty finding reliable information about traditional usage and maintenance
- Wellness claims that may mix traditional beliefs with scientific claims without clear distinction
- Limited visibility into the artisans and communities behind products
- Product information, heritage stories and care instructions being scattered across different sources
- Packaging that often ends the customer experience instead of extending it

Existing craft and e-commerce platforms help users discover and purchase traditional products, but ROOTS focuses on bringing the entire educational, cultural and purchasing journey into one connected experience.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

ROOTS creates an experience that goes beyond conventional e-commerce.

Instead of simply showing:

**Product → Price → Purchase**

ROOTS creates a journey centered around:

**Heritage → Material → Maker → Knowledge → Usage → Purchase → Continued Connection**

For example, consider a customer looking for a copper water bottle.

Instead of seeing only:

**Copper Water Bottle | ₹899 | Buy Now**

the customer can explore:

- The history and cultural significance of copper
- Traditional uses and practices
- Modern scientific evidence and relevant considerations
- The artisan and region associated with the product
- The craftsmanship and making process
- How to correctly use the product
- How to clean and maintain it
- Transparent product and pricing information

After purchase, a QR code on the packaging connects the physical product to its digital Heritage Passport, allowing the customer to continue exploring its story, maker, usage and care instructions.

# 🌿 Key Features

## 1. Material Discovery & Mission Page

ROOTS provides a dedicated educational space where users can explore heritage materials such as:

- Copper
- Brass
- Bronze
- Other traditionally crafted materials

Users can learn about their history, cultural relevance, traditional practices, craftsmanship and place in contemporary lifestyles.

The Mission Page also communicates the purpose of ROOTS and its approach to reviving traditional knowledge responsibly.

---

## 2. Tradition Meets Modern Evidence

ROOTS takes an education-first approach to wellness-related information.

Rather than presenting traditional claims as established scientific facts, information is clearly separated into:

### Traditional Wisdom
Information about traditional and Ayurvedic practices associated with the material.

### Modern Evidence
Information supported by available scientific research.

### Important Considerations
Relevant information users should know before using the product.

This allows ROOTS to respect traditional knowledge while encouraging informed decision-making.

---

## 3. Intelligent Product Discovery

Users can discover products through an advanced filtering system based on:

- Material
- Product category
- Price range
- Region
- Purpose
- Delivery preferences

ROOTS can also support natural-language product discovery, allowing users to search in a conversational way.

Example:

> "I want a traditional copper product for my kitchen under ₹1500."

The system can interpret the user's requirements and surface relevant products.

---

## 4. Immersive Product Detail Pages

ROOTS product pages go beyond standard e-commerce specifications.

Each product can include:

- Product name
- Price
- Exact material
- Dimensions
- Capacity where applicable
- Region of origin
- Traditional uses
- Cultural significance
- Craftsmanship information
- Artisan information
- Relevant wellness information
- Usage instructions
- Maintenance instructions
- Video demonstrations

The objective is to help users understand a product before purchasing it.

---

## 5. 📖 Heritage Passport

Each product can have a digital Heritage Passport containing its identity and story.

The Heritage Passport can include:

- Material
- Region
- Craft tradition
- Artisan/community
- Making process
- Historical context
- Traditional usage
- Care instructions
- Product-specific videos

The Heritage Passport can be accessed directly from the website or through a QR code printed on the physical packaging.

---

## 6. 👨‍🎨 Meet the Maker

ROOTS gives visibility to the people behind traditional products.

Product pages can introduce:

- Artisan profiles
- Artisan communities
- Craft traditions
- Region
- Making techniques
- Craft lineage
- The story behind the product

This creates a stronger connection between consumers and the craftsmanship behind the objects they purchase.

---

## 7. 🧼 Interactive Care Guide Library

Traditional materials often require specific care and maintenance.

ROOTS provides an interactive library containing:

- Cleaning instructions
- Maintenance methods
- Usage guidelines
- Things to avoid
- Product-specific care instructions
- Visual guides
- Artisan-created or curated videos

This helps customers confidently incorporate traditional products into everyday life.

---

## 8. 📦 Connected Sustainable Packaging

ROOTS treats packaging as an extension of the brand and digital experience rather than simply a protective container.

The packaging concept focuses on:

- Sustainable materials
- Minimal material usage
- Reusability where practical
- Contemporary interpretations of Indian heritage
- Clear product information
- QR-based digital experiences

A QR code on the package can take the customer directly to the product's Heritage Passport.

This creates a connection between the physical product and its digital story even after the purchase is complete.

---

## 9. 💰 Transparent Artisan Support

ROOTS aims to make the value chain more visible to customers.

Product pages can provide a transparent breakdown showing how the purchase price is distributed across areas such as:

- Artisan / cooperative
- Raw materials
- Sustainable packaging
- Platform and operations

Customers can also optionally add an artisan appreciation contribution during checkout, with the contribution directed to the artisan.

Actual price distributions will be based on verified vendor or product data rather than arbitrary fixed percentages.

# 🔄 User Journey

A typical ROOTS customer journey is:

1. The user discovers the ROOTS homepage.
2. The user explores a material such as copper, brass or bronze.
3. The user learns about its history, cultural significance and traditional usage.
4. The user browses relevant products.
5. The user applies filters or uses natural-language search.
6. The user opens a product's detailed page.
7. The user explores its heritage, artisan, craftsmanship and usage.
8. The user reviews care instructions and relevant evidence-based information.
9. The user adds the product to the cart.
10. The user completes checkout.
11. The customer receives the product in ROOTS sustainable packaging.
12. The customer scans the QR code.
13. The customer accesses the product's Heritage Passport and continues exploring its story and care information.

# 🔐 Security & Trust

The platform will follow basic security practices including:

- Secure authentication through Supabase
- Database access controls
- Row Level Security where applicable
- Environment variables for API keys
- No exposure of private API credentials in frontend code
- Validation of user inputs

For wellness-related content, ROOTS will clearly distinguish traditional knowledge from modern scientific evidence and avoid presenting unsupported claims as medical facts.
